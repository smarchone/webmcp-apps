# Notifications spec

## Goal

Let users know when something they care about changes, without flooding their inbox.

## Scope

- Email notifications for mentions and assigned tasks
- In-app notification center with unread counts
- Daily digest for everything else

## Requirements

1. Users get an email within 5 minutes of being mentioned.
2. Users can mute a project.
3. The notification center shows the last 50 notifications.
4. Digests go out every day at 9am.
